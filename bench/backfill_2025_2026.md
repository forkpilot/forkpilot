## Reading

- Copter: 6 of 6 localized findings are real behaviour changes in the code they name. 1 first-pass
  flag was dropped by triage. No false alarm reached the report.
- Plane: 3 real (one is a simulator change), 5 false alarms, 1 uncertain; 5 more first-pass
  flags dropped by triage. Every false alarm is a landing/touchdown metric or a QuadPlane mission
  leg metric, the same metrics that were noisy in the A/A runs (`bench/aa_scenarios.md`). When the
  flag is noise, bisect follows the noise to an unrelated commit.
- Per week: Copter about 1 false alarm per year, Plane about 1 every 10 weeks.
- holdout2 bugs in the window: `posctrl_down_sign_flip` and `qplane_spoolup_block_stuck` were fixed
  in the same PR (the weekly range has no net change, correctly no regression).
  `plane_training_shaping_stale` was found (2026-07-09 above). `loiter_coord_turn_brake` and
  `mis_options_bitmask` were introduced before the window.
- Not measured: regressions in the window that no scenario flies. A list of known regressions
  from the issue tracker is needed to count misses.
